Therapeutic Conflictology

Conflict-informed support for individuals, couples, families and professionals navigating emotional stress, family tension, career decisions and major life transitions.

Start the conversation

What it is

Understanding the conflict beneath the problem.

Therapeutic Conflictology helps you understand the conflict beneath what you are facing and create a practical path forward. It is supportive, strategic and grounded in emotional insight and conflict resolution.

This is not traditional therapy. Therapeutic Conflictology provides conflict-informed support to help you gain clarity, strengthen emotional resilience and move forward with practical next steps.

My approach

Practical, focused, and yours.

Sessions are goal-oriented and practical, often four to twelve meetings, focused on steadier emotional footing, clearer decisions and tools you can use between sessions.

The work draws on doctoral training in conflict analysis. Many clients are Caribbean or diaspora, navigating family expectations, migration and demanding careers while feeling privately depleted.

Support here focuses on you: emotional steadiness, co-parenting, burnout and grief while you lead at home and at work. Clinical mental health care for children is referred appropriately.

What you leave with

  • A clearer read on what is driving the stress, beneath the surface argument or symptom
  • Practical language and tools for boundaries and difficult conversations
  • A mapped next step you can act on between sessions
  • Greater steadiness when family or work pressure builds
  • Confidence to make decisions during transition, relocation or career change

Client experiences

Real shifts, real clarity.

How it works

Three simple stages.

1

Initial conversation

We clarify what you want to change, what conflict may be underneath it, and whether this work is the right fit, including referrals when needed.

2

Focused work

Short-term, goal-oriented sessions: understanding patterns, strengthening resilience and mapping practical next steps.

3

Tools you keep

You leave with strategies you can use between sessions. Clarity and movement, not endless talk without direction.
