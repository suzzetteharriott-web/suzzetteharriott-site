Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This website, suzzetteharriott.com, is operated by Dr. Suzzette A. Harriott, PhD, practising as Suzzette Harriott & Associates (“Dr. Harriott”, “we”, “us”). Dr. Harriott respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website handles and how. Personal data is handled in line with Jamaica’s Data Protection Act, 2020.

What this website collects

This website does not have accounts, logins, contact forms or a database. It does not collect personal information from visitors. To get in touch, you email Dr. Harriott directly.

If you email Dr. Harriott, she receives the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number and message.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry
  • To arrange and provide the services you request, such as Therapeutic Conflictology sessions or RESET enrolment

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing.

Confidentiality

Information shared with Dr. Harriott in the course of services is treated as confidential, within the limits explained to you at the start of our work together. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or health details in your first email.

Technical services

This website is served by a hosting provider, which processes basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages securely. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads. We do not use analytics or advertising tools.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete personal information Dr. Harriott holds about you by emailing contact@suzzetteharriott.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always appear on this page.
