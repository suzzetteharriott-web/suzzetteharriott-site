Who comes here

You may be doing well on paper and still feel out of step inside.

Illustrative, not exhaustive. If your situation is not listed, you are still welcome.

  • A life transition, relocation, divorce, grief or burnout, and you want grounded support
  • Anxiety, low mood or repeating patterns, and you want tools that fit real life
  • Parenting stress, family conflict, or feeling overwhelmed while holding everyone else together
  • Tension at work or in leadership that is following you home
  • A strained relationship with food, eating or how you see your body
  • You feel capable on paper but misaligned inside, and want clarity without being rushed

About

Dr. Suzzette A. Harriott, PhD

A therapeutic conflictologist whose work brings together psychology, conflict resolution and human resources. Her doctorate is in Conflict Analysis and Resolution, and her practice is grounded in clarity, dignity and practical guidance.

Based in Kingston, Jamaica, she works with clients across the Caribbean, the United States, the United Kingdom and beyond.
