About the practice

A practice for people in transition.

I work with people navigating conflict: within families, within relationships, at work, and within themselves. The aim is to bring clarity, healing and direction, wherever a person stands today.

What guides this practice.

Mission

To meet people where they are.

Every person arrives carrying something different. The work begins with understanding what that is, without rushing to a label.

Vision

A region of considered care.

A Caribbean where psychological support is accessible, dignified and globally credible, and where seeking help carries the same weight as seeing a doctor.

Values

Honesty, depth, alignment.

Considered care over rushed advice. Rigour over surface coaching. Cultural rootedness over imported frameworks. The client’s own voice over my interpretation of it. Always.

Biography

Dr. Suzzette A. Harriott, PhD

Dr. Suzzette A. Harriott is a therapeutic conflictologist whose work brings together psychology, human resources and conflict resolution.

Her academic foundation includes undergraduate training in psychology, graduate training in human resource management, and a PhD in Conflict Analysis and Resolution. This gives her work a rare blend of behavioural insight, organisational understanding and practical strategy.

Her Therapeutic Conflictology work focuses on intrapersonal conflict, interpersonal conflict, relationship coaching, confidence building and practical support through transition. She also designs and facilitates RESET, an experiential growth programme for adults.

Recognised by Marquis Who’s Who for her contributions to conflict resolution, Dr. Harriott’s practice is grounded in clarity, dignity and practical guidance for individuals, couples and families navigating important decisions.

Based in Kingston, Jamaica, her practice serves clients across the Caribbean, the United States, the United Kingdom and beyond.
